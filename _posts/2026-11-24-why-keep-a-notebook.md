A notebook gives unfinished ideas somewhere to wait. It does not need a grand purpose. It only needs to be easy to return to.

## Keep the useful details

A few lines from a conversation or a question you have not answered can become useful later. Writing them down makes it easier to notice patterns and follow a thought further.

## Leave room to change your mind

An entry does not need to be polished or permanent. It can be a snapshot of what seemed interesting at the time.
